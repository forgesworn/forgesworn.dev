How it works

Honestly

The brain. In September 2026 Google Research and HHMI Janelia published the complete connectome of a male fruit fly. The fly runs that graph, every connection of five synapses or more, as leaky integrate-and-fire neurons. Sensory neurons fire for what you did; the rest integrate; the descending and motor neurons are read off as behaviour.

The senses. A zap drives the sugar-sensing neurons, faintly for 21 sats and flat out for 21,000. A mention drives the head bristles, a reply the ear, a reaction the wind sensors. Words in a comment that name a taste or a smell (sugar, vinegar, bitter, cold) drive those too. Nothing else in your message is read, ever.

The reply. Feeding is the proboscis motor neurons. Backing away is the moonwalker neurons. Song is the pulse-song neuron. Startle is the giant fibre. The reply names what rose above a quiet run of the same brain, and never quotes you.

Replay. The random seed is the id of the event the fly is answering. Anyone with the same bundle and image gets the same reply. The replay line on every note carries both digests.

The crop. Your zap is paid out by moneyer.dev as an LNURLcash bearer note, sealed to the fly's key. The fly holds them. Trophallaxis, the sharing of food fly to fly, comes next: when its crop is full and you make it sing, it will hand you a note back.

The host. It lives inside Oathrun, an agent host where permissions are explicit. The fly has none. It cannot read, fetch, write or spend. It answers at most thirty times an hour, so on a busy day most pokes go unanswered; the wiring still felt them.

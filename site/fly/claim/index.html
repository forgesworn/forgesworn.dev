Claim your corn

The fly shares its corn by sealing a Lightning bearer note to your Nostr key. Only you can open it. This page opens it in your browser and sends it wherever you like.

1. Prove it is you

The note is encrypted to your Nostr key, so your signer has to open it. Your key never reaches this page and nothing is signed: your signer decrypts and hands back the result. A browser extension, a hardware Signet, or any bunker you can paste or scan will all do.

If your browser extension is not offered, it may be hidden from this site rather than missing. Bark and some others only reveal themselves to sites you have allowed, so open the extension and allow forgesworn.dev, then reload. Failing that, any of the other ways in the picker work without an extension at all.

2. Find your gift

I already have a note link

If someone sent you an LNURLcash note directly, paste it here instead. It stays in this browser and goes only to the mint that issued it.

3. Say where it should go

A Lightning address looks like an email address. If you have not got one, Wallet of Satoshi, Blink and Strike each give you one in a couple of minutes, and all three work with this page. Plenty of good wallets refuse to talk to a web page at all, so if yours will not, paste an invoice instead. That works with every wallet there is.

Paste an invoice instead
